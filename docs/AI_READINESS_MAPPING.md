# ITU AI Readiness Framework 2.0 Mapping

Source: `SRC-ITU-002`. This is a scoped self-assessment, not a complete
13-dimension national readiness assessment.

| Relevant framework area | Candidate evidence | Status | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data accessibility and provenance | Fixture and source manifests are local, versioned and hashed | `PARTIAL` | Corpus breadth, multilingual material and external discoverability are limited |
| Data quality, timeliness and consistency | Sequence guards, domain-payload binding, late-event preservation, evidence timestamps and candidate freshness checks | `PARTIAL` | 300-second window is `PROPOSED_NOT_VALIDATED`; no field pilot |
| Data governance | Admission rules, source hashes, license disposition, synthetic-only data | `PARTIAL` | License and legal review incomplete |
| AI policy and regulation | UAE data, digital government and AI Charter claims are mapped to behavior | `PARTIAL` | No complete federal/emirate policy coverage or legal interpretation |
| Supervision and implementation guidance | Fail-closed authorization, human-oversight label and independent verifier CLI | `PARTIAL` | No production identity, ACL, external signer or human acceptance |
| AI for inclusion | No real personal data; privacy and no-authority controls are explicit | `PARTIAL` | Accessibility, Arabic UX and beneficiary coverage are not implemented |
| Granular priorities/local context | UAE heat-continuity fixture and official UAE source metadata | `PARTIAL` | Scenario status is `WORKING_ASSUMPTION_NOT_OWNER_APPROVED`; it is not validated with institutions |
| Digital infrastructure and energy | S2/S5 explicitly distinguish power from service recovery | `PARTIAL` | No real sensors, edge compute, energy measurement, uptime or coverage pilot |
| Gap identification | Standards, implementation and policy gaps are listed in this repository | `COMPLETED_LOCAL_SCOPE` | Gaps are not externally adjudicated |
