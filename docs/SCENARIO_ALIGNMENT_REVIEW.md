# Heat-Continuity Scenario Alignment Review

## Finding

The current scenario is a credible synthetic public-service continuity testbed,
but it is not evidence of a real UAE heat incident, a verified public-cooling
service, or a UAE heat-service operating mandate.

| Check | Result | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Domain problem is visible | `VERIFIED` | Heat, power, facility, route, demand and recovery uncertainty are linked in S1-S5 |
| Synthetic status is explicit | `VERIFIED` | Fixture, report and runtime record synthetic data and zero real authority |
| Unsupported UAE event claim | `VERIFIED` absent | No event is presented as a real UAE occurrence |
| UAE policy claim scope | `VERIFIED` scoped | General strategy, privacy, responsible AI, risk, coordination and responsibility claims only |
| Sector operating mandate | `BLOCKED` | No verified heat/public-cooling continuity mandate or emirate operating policy |
| Institutional roles and permissions | `COMPLETED` at fictional local level | Four roles have different functions and authority labels |
| Different information by role | `PARTIAL` | Outputs differ, but all roles are orchestrated from the same stage context |
| Progressive event release | `VERIFIED` | S1-S5, route gap, late arrival and time-indexed action requests |
| Institutional/technical/standards gaps | `COMPLETED` for local candidate | Authority, freshness, source, role, corpus and standard limitations are recorded |
| Global comparative cases | `BLOCKED` | No verified global policy-case corpus is included |

## Minimum Positioning

1. Present the scenario as a synthetic evaluation of continuity evidence and
   coordination, not as a UAE factual incident or sector policy implementation.
2. Keep all operational proposals side-effect free and fail closed.
3. Treat current UAE sources as cross-cutting governance guidance, not heat
   service authority.
4. State the missing heat-sector mandate and global comparative cases as
   material evaluation gaps.
5. Scenario selection remains a working assumption under ODR-01. Retain the
   fictional non-institutional coordinator boundary under ODR-02 and do not
   claim Owner approval or a real government mandate.
